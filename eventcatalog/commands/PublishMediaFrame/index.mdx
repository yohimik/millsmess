## Responsibility

Producer: **client**. Consumer: **MediaEngine**. Delivery: **realtime**. Channel: **MediaFrames**.

## Preconditions and effects

Validate authenticated participant, publish permission, epoch and connection generation before accepting a frame. Codec manifest defines packetization and timestamp units. Reject oversize frames; enforce congestion/bitrate budgets and drop expired timestamps. No durable ACK or historical replay. RTP tracks use SRTP directly and never this JSON envelope.

## Ordering and idempotency

Session + owner epoch + player + connection generation + logical channel. Sequence numbers are local to this scope.

See the domain delivery contract for replay windows, payload-hash conflicts and admission expiry.

## Failure handling

Drop expired, duplicate or unauthorized frames; rate-limit and disconnect abusive senders. Missing snapshots trigger resync, not unlimited retransmission. No durable delivery guarantee applies.
