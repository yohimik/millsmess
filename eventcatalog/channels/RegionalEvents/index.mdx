Broker-neutral at-least-once durable lifecycle stream. Reference adapter: regional NATS JetStream or Kafka with persisted acknowledgements and per-aggregate routing. Ordered per producer-owned aggregate only. Core subscribers use competing workers; Socket fanout must reach each interested gateway, not just one random consumer. Topic ACLs bind allowed producer/type. Seven-day retention, eight-day inbox dedup; see domain redrive contract.
