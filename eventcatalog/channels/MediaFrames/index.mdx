Optional negotiated audio/video codec frames over the common data-adapter interface. PublishMediaFrame/MediaFrame carry timestamps, sequence and codec payload; enforce full encoded-message size budgets and encryption overhead. Opus packets may fit datagrams; oversized video requires reliable transport or standard RTP packetization, never implicit v1 UDP fragmentation. Unsupported codec packetization is refused. This is ephemeral live traffic, not a broker topic.
