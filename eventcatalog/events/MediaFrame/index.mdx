Forward an authorized codec frame over WSS, data channel or secure UDP.

## Responsibility

Producer: **MediaEngine**. Consumer: **client**. Delivery: **realtime**. Channel: **MediaFrames**.

## Preconditions and effects

Per-recipient subscription and privacy checks precede forwarding. mediaTimestamp/timescale drive playout; sequence orders one channel generation. Receivers drop expired frames and use a bounded jitter buffer. kind and codec must equal ChannelOpened; keyFrame marks decoder recovery points. Missing video data waits for the next keyframe; transport adapter codec policy handles refresh requests.

## Ordering and idempotency

Session + owner epoch + player + connection generation + logical channel. Sequence numbers are local to this scope.

See the domain delivery contract for replay windows, payload-hash conflicts and admission expiry.

## Failure handling

Drop expired, duplicate or unauthorized frames; rate-limit and disconnect abusive senders. Missing snapshots trigger resync, not unlimited retransmission. No durable delivery guarantee applies.

An event is a fact or scoped reply; its receipt does not bypass authorization or ownership validation.
