Publish a durable terminal watermark for one session-event source stream.

## Responsibility

Producer: **Core, Socket, MediaEngine**. Consumer: **Core**. Delivery: **durable**. Channel: **SessionRecordingStream**.

## Preconditions and effects

After its last event and all media finalization metadata are durable, a writer atomically closes its session-scoped stream. Repeated same watermark/digest is idempotent; no later event may append to a closed stream. Core reconciles contiguous sequence, count and digest before sealing the session archive. This recorder receipt is not recursively recorded inside the stream it closes.
